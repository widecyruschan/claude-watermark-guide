Practical Takeaways

  • Invisible Unicode characters are not Claude's official text watermark.
  • Short, factual, code-heavy, or lightly proofread passages may contain too little signal for reliable detection.
  • Heavy rewriting can weaken the signal, but this site does not provide watermark removal or bypass tools.
  • A detected mark cannot establish authorship, ownership, or whether Claude wrote rather than edited the text.
