Cookie Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

Draft status: This policy is provided for product and legal review and is not legal advice.

Scope

This policy explains how Watermark Lens uses cookies and similar browser storage on watermarklens.com.

Necessary Storage

The public local character tools do not require first-party cookies. Cloudflare or other security providers may set strictly necessary cookies when required to protect and deliver the site. When account features are enabled, authentication may use secure browser storage or strictly necessary session mechanisms to keep you signed in, refresh a session, prevent abuse, and complete OAuth redirects.

Analytics

We use a Plausible-compatible analytics service hosted at plausible.shipsolo.io. The configured script measures aggregate traffic without analytics cookies or persistent cross-site identifiers. Because no optional analytics cookie is currently set, the site does not show an analytics-cookie consent banner.

Third-Party Services

Google OAuth, Stripe-hosted checkout or billing pages, and links to external services may set cookies on their own domains. Their cookies are controlled by those providers and governed by their policies, not by this site's cookie settings.

Managing Cookies and Storage

You can inspect, block, or delete cookies and site storage through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary security or authentication storage may prevent account sign-in, OAuth, payment, or protected access from working.

Changes

If we introduce optional analytics, advertising, or personalization cookies, we will update this policy and provide consent controls where required before using them.
